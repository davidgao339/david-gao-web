Hard Constraints (Strict Feasibility)

  • • Store Coverage Demand: Exactly N baristas and supervisors present during opening, peak rush, and closing hours.
  • • Rest Interval: Minimum 12 hours of mandatory rest between consecutive shifts for any staff member.
  • • Weekly Max Hours: Strict cap at 40 hours/week per employee to prevent overtime penalties under labor laws.
  • • Single Assignment: No employee can be assigned to multiple stores or overlapping slots on the same day.

Objective Function & Soft Penalties

  • • Fair Weekend Allocation: Minimizes the variance of weekend shifts across the employee roster.
  • • Commute Distance Minimization: Prioritizes branch assignments closest to employee residential zones.
  • • Availability Preferences: Soft bonus for honoring voluntary time-off and preferred shift requests.
  • • Solver Speed: High-efficiency boolean satisfiability tree traversal finds optimal assignments in < 180s.
