Hard Constraints (Strict Feasibility)

The solver rejects any candidate schedule violating even one hard constraint, guaranteeing legal compliance and complete physical coverage.

  • •
    Store Coverage Demand:

    Exactly 2 baristas present for morning prep, 3 staff during peak midday rush, and 2 staff for closing reconciliation.

  • •
    Mandatory Rest Interval:

    Strict minimum of 12 continuous rest hours between shifts. A barista finishing an evening shift at 22:00 cannot open at 08:00.

  • •
    Weekly Maximum Hours Cap:

    Absolute cap at 40 hours per week per employee, eliminating unwanted overtime expenses and labor law penalties.

  • •
    Single Branch Assignment:

    No employee can be assigned to multiple branches or conflicting overlapping slots on the same calendar day.

Objective Function & Fairness Optimization

Soft constraints guide the solver toward the highest quality, most equitable schedule through weighted penalty minimization.

  • •
    Weekend Fairness Balancing:

    Minimizes the variance of Saturday and Sunday shifts across staff, ensuring no team member is disproportionately scheduled on weekends.

  • •
    Commute Distance Minimization:

    Weights staff-to-branch residential zones so employees are assigned to the closest available retail locations.

  • •
    Availability & PTO Respect:

    Applies heavy reward bonuses for honoring voluntary shift preferences and pre-approved time-off requests.

  • •
    Rapid Tree Traversal:

    Google OR-Tools boolean satisfiability tree search finds provably optimal schedule solutions in < 2 seconds.
