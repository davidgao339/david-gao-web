I'm a data and operations guy who gets genuinely excited about automation. My thing is taking the boring, repetitive work off people's plates — the manual scheduling, the payroll calculations, the copy-pasting — so they can focus on the decisions that actually matter.

I use whatever tools get the job done: Python, AI agents, React, SQL, Databricks. And if I don't know how? I'll figure it out. That's probably my biggest strength — I've never met a problem I couldn't eventually solve, even if it meant learning something entirely new to get there.

Outside of work I'm building Lego, dabbling in options trading (most of my money is safely in ETFs, don't worry), and reading when I can — always open to book recommendations. I studied finance and worked in wealth management, so investing genuinely fascinates me.
